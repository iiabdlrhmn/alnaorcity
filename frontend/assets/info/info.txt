Our Vision:
To become a leading regional company in building local brands and connecting global brands with markets across the region.

Our Mission:
To develop distinctive local brands, represent trusted international brands, and deliver quality products that meet market needs through a deep understanding of consumers and a commitment to sustainable growth.

Business Goals & Objectives:
- Brand Development: Build and grow a strong portfolio of distinctive local brands that respond to evolving consumer needs.
- Market Expansion: Strengthen the company’s presence in Iraq and expand into selected regional markets.
- International Partnerships: Establish strategic partnerships with reputable international brands to bring quality products to local and regional markets.
- Portfolio Diversification: Expand across complementary food and beverage categories to address a wider range of market opportunities.
- Market Leadership: Build competitive advantages through product quality, consumer understanding, and effective market positioning.
- Sustainable Growth: Develop scalable business operations that support long-term growth, operational efficiency, and lasting value.

Our History:
Company History
 Al-Naor City began its journey in the wholesale food trading sector in 1997 under the name Sayed ugla Office, operating in Jamila Industrial Area through a Cash & Carry business model. This approach enabled the company to establish a strong foothold in the Iraqi market.
In 2021, the company entered a new phase of growth to FMCG with its official registration and the launch of its flagship brand, Al-Khan. This marked a fundamental shift from traditional trading towards developing and building its own consumer brands.
Since then,
Al-Naor City has expanded its product portfolio across various food categories, including tea, rice, olive oil, and rice bran oil, while developing additional brands to address the diverse needs of the market. Al-Khan has also evolved beyond packaged products to offer a broader consumer experience through the establishment of Shai Al-Khan, a spacious café located in Baghdad’s Jadriya district.
Building on its deep understanding of the Iraqi market and expertise in brand development,
Al-Naor City aims to strengthen its role as a bridge between local brand innovation and international market access. 
The company represents its own brands alongside international brands, guided by a long-term vision to expand its presence across multiple regional markets.
